How engagements work: choose, approve, then build

1. Tell us what you want: Select the package that fits your goal and describe the result you need in the estimate request. We review the scope, deliverables, timeline, and final price together.

2. Approve before paying: You receive a written estimate and agreement identifying exactly what will be built or delivered. Nothing starts until you approve and sign.

3. Pay 50% to begin: After signing, a 50% deposit is due before work starts. The remaining 50% is due upon completion, before final delivery, unless the signed agreement states otherwise. Payments are arranged through the approved GoHighLevel process, not collected on this page. Read refund and cancellation terms.

4. We build and review: We complete the approved work, review results with you, and hand over the agreed deliverables after final payment.
