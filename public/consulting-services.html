All 12 AI Boss Mobility Services

A clear description of every service: what is included, what the customer receives, the work AI performs, when it can be delivered, and how completion is checked. The seven established services are listed first, followed by five new AI consulting services.

Your approval is the decision point

Following the paid $149 Clarity & Quick Start meeting, customers choose deliverables. AI prepares the scope and feasibility review; Brian approves or declines the project. After the customer signs the approved agreement and the first 50% payment is independently verified, AI may complete permitted production work. Documented quality checks must pass before the final 50% invoice is requested; finished files are released only after final payment is verified.

Some live account changes, customer communications, technical integrations and training events require explicit permissions or a human facilitator. AI sends exceptions to Brian instead of silently changing scope or price. This describes the intended workflow; payment verification, production automation and release gates are still being configured and tested.

One process for all twelve

  1. Pay for and complete the initial $149 session; do not book until the full $149 is verified.
  2. Select requested deliverables; AI checks scope, tools, permissions, feasibility, estimated completion date and price.
  3. Brian approves or declines the written project scope.
  4. Customer signs the agreement and pays the verified 50% deposit before production begins.
  5. AI builds the approved deliverables, tests them and records work evidence in the job record; unresolved issues are escalated.
  6. After completion tests pass, send a progress summary and request the final 50%.
  7. Only after final payment is verified, release accepted deliverables and retain delivery confirmation.

Job record: selected checklist, written scope, agreement, approvals, payment references, work files, test evidence, exceptions, final payment, and delivery confirmation.
