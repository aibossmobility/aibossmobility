Common questions and practical starting points

What should introductory AI training cover?

Begin with realistic tasks, AI limitations, privacy, fact-checking, and hands-on practice. Learning to evaluate output matters more than memorizing prompts.

How do we avoid overwhelming instructors?

Pick one or two common workflows, set clear safeguards and give people a manageable practice environment with time for questions.

How do we know whether our program is ready?

Consider learner goals, accessibility, available devices, staff skills, existing software, and the kinds of work participants plan to do.

Can we request training or a workforce proposal?

Yes. Select an existing education or strategy service and save an organizational project brief. Customized work may require a separate estimate before payment.
