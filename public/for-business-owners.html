Common questions and practical starting points

How could AI help with customer follow-up?

Document how inquiries arrive and who replies today. Then consider a small, approved follow-up or task-reminder process. Keep people responsible for consent, sensitive messages and relationship-building.

Do I need to buy another tool?

Often not. Begin with the systems you already use, identify the bottleneck and test whether a practical change improves the work before spending more.

How do I know whether automation will save money?

Include software fees, setup, human review, maintenance, and potential mistakes when comparing costs and benefits. Savings depend on your situation and are not guaranteed.
