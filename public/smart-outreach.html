Who this is for

Technology should make room for people.

Whether you lead a growing business, nonprofit, church, or training organization, the aim is the same: less time lost between receiving a request and taking a responsible next step.

No specific software subscription is required just to discuss your needs. We begin by evaluating what you already use and what permissions or safeguards your organization needs.

What we won't promise

No guaranteed lead counts, sales or response rates; no fabricated relationships; no bulk unsolicited outreach; no automatic customer charges; and no claim that an integration works before it has passed its tests.

Your organization stays in control. You approve consequential messages, costs, and access to sensitive information.

Common questions

Can you use our current email and customer tools?

That's the starting point. We'll review your approved tools, permissions, privacy requirements and capabilities before proposing a solution.

No. The process emphasizes legitimate inquiries, clear permission, accurate records, and appropriate responses. Any outbound activity follows your approved scope and applicable communication requirements.

No. The setup stands on its own, subject to any third-party services your configuration depends on. Optional support is quoted and agreed separately.

When do we pay?

After reviewing and approving a written scope, you receive a 50% setup deposit request. The matching agreement follows independently verified payment, and work begins only after both records match. The final 50% invoice requires independent QA, preview and explicit acceptance. An estimate request never charges you.
