My payment succeeded. What happens next?

Keep the payment receipt and signed agreement. Consulting services follow the scope, milestones, payment schedule, and delivery method in that agreement. Payment alone does not book a meeting or confirm that a project has started. If your confirmation or next step is missing, choose Payment or booking below.

My payment failed, is pending, or I may have paid twice.

Check your receipt and payment status before retrying. A pending authorization is not confirmed payment. If you see a completed charge, do not submit another payment. Send the receipt or order number below so Brian can check it. Never send your card number, security code, password, or bank details.

I need a meeting time or need to reschedule.

Use the reschedule link in your confirmed booking email. If there is no confirmed booking, request booking help below and include your receipt number and preferred availability. A request does not reserve a time; Brian must confirm it.

Where are my consulting deliverables?

Use the delivery method and deadline in your signed agreement. Check your inbox and spam folder. Consulting is a service, so it does not automatically unlock Papa Life's learning library. Select Missing deliverables and identify the agreed milestone.

I entered the wrong email, need a correction, or want a refund.

Choose the issue below and include the receipt/order number. Brian verifies the payment and identity before changing records or reviewing a refund. Requests are not automatically approved. See the payment and cancellation terms and your signed agreement.
