What this report does and does not measure

It counts JavaScript-enabled page loads, interest clicks and confirmed intake acknowledgements. It does not count unique people, actual digital-twin conversations, booked consultations, or paid customers. Automated scans are filtered where identifiable, but some nonhuman traffic can remain.

Daily aggregates are stored only on the existing Railway volume, without cookies, names, email addresses, IP addresses or persistent tracking IDs. Counts are retained for up to 90 days.
