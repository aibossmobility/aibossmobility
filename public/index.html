Innovation Has Always Been Ours Too

From the traffic signal to the AI age: learn the rules, read the signals, keep moving.

In 1923, Black inventor Garrett Morgan patented an improved three-position traffic signal with an important pause between stop and go. AI Boss Mobility uses that same idea as a teaching metaphor for this new technology era: know when to stop, when to slow down and learn, and when to move forward.

This is especially for older adults, including Black and Latino baby boomers, who may feel technology moved too fast without them. You are not too late. You do not have to become a programmer. You need a trusted bridge, understandable language and a practical next step.

The AI traffic-light rule

RED — STOP: Stop guessing, overspending and chasing tools.

YELLOW — CAUTION: Stay where you are long enough to understand what you are doing.

GREEN — COME ON IN: When you are clear, move forward. Learn, build and let the Digital Twin help guide you.

Technology changes. The wisdom to stop, look, learn and proceed still matters.
