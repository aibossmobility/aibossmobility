What managed hosting includes

Initial hosting configuration for the agreed website, domain/DNS connection assistance, HTTPS setup, publication and routine hosting status checks. The included hosting allowance is limited to standard small-business usage and a suitable provider selected in the written agreement. Domain registration, business email, paid plugins, CRM subscriptions, high-traffic usage, custom databases, payment systems and other third-party charges are separate. We do not activate billable upgrades without written authorization.

You own the business assets. We recommend that customers own their domain and any lead database. AI Boss Mobility can be delegated technical access without mixing customer contacts into our own CRM. We will document website handoff and cancellation procedures.

Monthly time allowances do not roll over. Extra approved work is quoted at $75/hour. Neither support nor hosting is purchased by submitting an inquiry. A separate written agreement and payment authorization are required.
