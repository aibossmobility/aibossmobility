Let's Practice Critical Thinking

AI can be helpful, convincing—and wrong. These examples come from Brian Keith Hill's real work with AI. Your job isn't to disagree automatically. Your job is to ask the right questions and verify the result.

What would you ask or do next?

Your four critical-thinking questions

  1. Why? Why did AI make that choice?
  2. How? What facts or evidence support the answer?
  3. Does it match? Is this the result I actually asked for?
  4. Prove it. Can I see and test what is complete?

Keep critically thinking for yourself when using AI. You remain responsible for your decisions.
