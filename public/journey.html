The Work Did Not Start With the Hype

Brian Keith Hill's Technology & AI Journey

AI Boss Mobility did not appear overnight. It grew out of years of online teaching, website building, digital marketing, entrepreneurship education, community service, experimentation and continuous learning.

Why this page exists: When people ask why they should trust Brian to help them cross into AI, the answer is not “because AI is popular.” The record shows a long progression of learning technology, teaching others, building digital systems and applying new tools to real human problems.
2006–2009

Online teaching before today's AI wave

Brian taught online courses as an adjunct professor at California State University, East Bay. That experience helped form an early belief that technology could extend teaching beyond a physical room.

PUBLIC 2022 Life Coach Code profile documenting the earlier online-teaching history.
2011–2015

Building websites, social presence and digital community

Brian supported minority-owned businesses with search and social media marketing and built and maintained the Black Men Speak website and social presence in Oakland. The pattern was already clear: learn the technology, then use it to help people be seen and connected.

PUBLIC Life Coach Code profile.
2018

“The Apexboss innovates again!”

While participating in Marisa Murgatroyd's Experience Product Masterclass through Live Your Message, Brian defined an Experience Product focused on online marketing and coaching for Black and Latino Father Entrepreneurs. His milestone work repeatedly described teaching fathers to use software tools to generate new customers.
