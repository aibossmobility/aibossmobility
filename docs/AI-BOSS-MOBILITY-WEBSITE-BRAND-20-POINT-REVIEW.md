# AI Boss Mobility Consulting — 20-Point Website Brand Review

**Status:** Working application of an existing 20-point design/asset governance guide, **not** a new logo, a visitor-facing 20-option menu, or a replacement for the original Papa Life standard.

**Important origin:** The numbered 1–20 source was the **Papa Life Brand Asset Governance / Master Naming and Visual Map**. Its course titles, fatherhood messaging, and Papa Life product requirements remain Papa Life-specific. The 20 points below adapt its relevant review principles to AI Boss Mobility Consulting. They do not claim there is an independently approved 20-point AI Boss standard.

**Website direction approved October 22, 2026:** Warm middle beige (`#D9C3A3`), large human photographs that transition gently, simple welcoming text, clear CTA, two or three choices at a time, older-adult readability, Black and Latino cultural representation, and one message/decision at a time. Keep the existing website's services, educational content, policy material, legal copy, consent, estimate-before-invoice path, and Brian's AI Twin accessible on appropriate deeper pages. Distinguish AI Boss Mobility Consulting from Papa Life.

## Twenty review selections (each must be checked, not all shown to visitors)

1. **Brand purpose:** Clarify AI and support human understanding and wise decisions; do not substitute Papa Life family-reconnection copy.
2. **Customer journey:** Move from confusion to clarity to informed, voluntary decisions; no pressure.
3. **Audience:** Include older adults and Black/Latino entrepreneurs, business owners, churches, nonprofits, and community leaders in a dignified manner.
4. **Visual atmosphere:** Human, modern, warm, spacious, readable; avoid sterile dark interfaces, clutter, or generic tech imagery.
5. **Page structure:** Brand identity, one emotionally appropriate large image, one concise message, one unmistakable primary action.
6. **Exact naming:** Use **AI Boss Mobility Consulting**, correct page titles and established service names.
7. **Service labeling:** Distinguish educational resources, interactive guidance, services, custom estimates, and AI Twin assistance accurately.
8. **Accurate CTA:** Every displayed button must lead to the described next step; no misleading purchase or enrollment language.
9. **Visual consistency:** Maintain a coherent family of images and typography across homepage and deeper pages; do not copy Papa Life course-cover conventions.
10. **Master content inventory:** Preserve and reconcile live content, existing URLs, policy text, service names, and status before rearranging.
11. **Page-specific imagery:** Match each service or educational topic with appropriate people/activity and context, rather than repeated generic laptop scenes.
12. **Cultural representation:** Respect Black and Latino cultures with non-stereotyped, believable people and settings. Follow the user's current imagery preferences.
13. **Emotional alignment:** Show curiosity, warmth, confidence, trust, collaboration, and agency; avoid staged boardroom crowds.
14. **Sizing and cropping:** Design responsive widescreen and small-screen layouts with protected faces, readable text, and images that do not crop subjects awkwardly.
15. **Asset filenames:** Keep originals, provide stable descriptive filenames and document source, rights, and alternative text.
16. **Quality review:** Test titles, labels, cultural representation, content consistency, contrast, mobile readability, navigation, link targets, reduced motion, and CTA behavior.
17. **End-to-end consistency:** Service names and prices must match service pages and written-estimate flow; never request invoices/payments for custom work ahead of acceptance.
18. **Trustworthy identity:** Preserve approved logo artwork when used; do not substitute text-based or AI-generated approximations. Brand palette is not limited to four flat color swatches; black, white, red, yellow, green, and the approved warm beige have contextual roles.
19. **Implementation order:** Build/test the experience page by page, preserve previous live copy and routes, and verify new paths before replacing production.
20. **Operating rule:** Never publish invented client testimonials, outcomes, live-video claims, service availability, costs, signed agreements, or unverified integrations.

## What visitors actually see

- **First screen:** Large scene, concise existing message, one large Start Here button.
- **Second screen:** Maximum three approachable routes. Use existing choices: Something is stuck; Help me understand my options; I'm ready to move.
- **Third and later screens:** Reveal smaller, relevant subsets of the existing lessons, options, services, estimates, policy pages, and AI Twin after the visitor selects a path.
- **Accessibility:** Pause/disable motion under reduced-motion settings and provide readable captions and real button labels.
