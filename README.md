Papa Life brings together fatherhood, personal growth, and responsible AI to strengthen the relationships that matter most. I build human-centered tools and experiences that help fathers reconnect with themselves, show up for their families, and use technology without losing the human connection at the center of life.

### What guides the work

- **Fatherhood:** presence, responsibility, healing, and purpose
- **Responsible AI:** technology that supports human agency, dignity, and trust
- **Human connection:** real relationships before metrics, automation, or scale
- **Practical growth:** tools that turn insight into meaningful daily action
